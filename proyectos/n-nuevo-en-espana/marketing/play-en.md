## Full description (max. 4000 characters)

```
Nuevo en España explains official procedures in Spain step by step, in plain language, for people who have just arrived or just got legal status.

IMPORTANT: Nuevo en España is an independent app. It does not represent any government entity and is not affiliated with the Government of Spain, any ministry, the Policía Nacional, the Seguridad Social or any town hall. The information comes from official public sources (BOE, ministries, Seguridad Social, DGT, town halls and others), which are linked in every guide with the date they were checked. It is general information, not legal advice. We do not book appointments (cita previa) and we do not carry out procedures for you.

WHAT YOU GET IN EACH GUIDE
• Who the procedure is for
• Key facts: cost, time, where to apply
• Steps in order
• Documents you need
• Deadlines and fees, with the official form (for example modelo 790 código 052)
• Common mistakes and how to avoid them
• A checklist to tick off what you already have
• Email reminders before your deadlines
• Differences between regions (for example Madrid and Barcelona)
• Links to the official sources

THE 8 GUIDES
• Empadronamiento: register your address with the town hall. Also without a rental contract. This guide is free.
• Renew your residence permit, including the 1-year permit from the 2026 regularisation (Real Decreto 316/2026), which is modified, not renewed
• Public health card (tarjeta sanitaria), also without legal residence (Real Decreto 180/2026)
• Social Security number (NUSS): free, online in Import@ss or at a TGSS office
• Basic bank account (cuenta de pago básica): your right to open one, maximum 3 € a month
• Driving licence: exchange (canje) or theory test at the DGT
• Recognition of your degree or diploma (homologación or equivalencia)
• Spanish nationality by residence: years needed, CCSE and DELE A2 exams, fee

Official Spanish names stay in Spanish, with a short explanation in English, so you recognise them at the office: empadronamiento, NIE, TIE, cita previa, volante, certificado, Registro Civil, Jefatura de Tráfico and more.

REMINDERS SO YOU DO NOT MISS A DEADLINE
Enter a date (for example, when your TIE card expires) and we email you before the key moments. For a residence permit renewal, you can apply from 60 days before expiry up to 90 days after. We remind you when the period opens and before it closes.

LANGUAGES
The app is available in 7 languages: Spanish, English, Arabic, French, Romanian, Chinese and Ukrainian.

FREE AND PAID CONTENT
Free: the full empadronamiento guide and the summary of every guide.
With a subscription or a 12-month pack: all full guides, checklists and deadline reminders.

OUR RULES
• Every deadline and fee links to an official source, with the date it was checked
• If we could not verify something, we say so and tell you where to check
• We never charge for appointments: official appointments are free and booked only on official websites
• No personal legal advice

For your specific case, contact a lawyer or a free support organisation for migrants.

Contact: hola@numimates.com
```

## Notes for the team (not part of the listing)

- The declaration that the app does not represent any government entity and that the information comes from linked official sources is mandatory: keep it in the first lines of the full description, where it is visible without tapping "more". It must also appear in the app (see `aviso.largo` in `content/ui/en.json`).
- Do not use government logos, coats of arms or flags in the icon, screenshots or feature graphic.
- In the Play Console, under "Government apps", declare that the app is not a government app, and fill the "App content > Financial features" and "Data safety" sections (email for reminders).
- Prices in the app (4,99 €/month, 19,90 € for 12 months) are currently paid through Stripe on the website. Before publishing on Google Play, check whether the Payments policy requires Google Play Billing for this digital content inside the app.
- Before publishing, check the length with the Play Console counter (limits: 30 / 80 / 4000 characters).
