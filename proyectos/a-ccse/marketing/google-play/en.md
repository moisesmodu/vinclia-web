## Descripción completa (2725/4000 caracteres; objetivo 2500-3800)

```text
Applying for Spanish citizenship by residence? First you need to pass the CCSE test (Constitutional and Sociocultural Knowledge of Spain), run by the Instituto Cervantes. CCSE en tu idioma helps you prepare with all 300 official questions and explains every answer in your own language.

WHAT THE EXAM LOOKS LIKE
• 25 questions taken from the public bank of 300 in the official 2026 manual.
• 45 minutes. You pass with 15 correct answers (60%). Wrong answers are not penalised.
• The exam is always in Spanish. That is why we explain each question in English while you practise with the original Spanish text.
• 5 sections: government and civic participation; rights and duties; territory and geography; culture and history; Spanish society.

WHO NEEDS IT
Foreign nationals applying for Spanish citizenship by residence. Note: people from Spanish-speaking countries must also take the CCSE (they are only exempt from the DELE A2). Exempt: under-18s and anyone who completed compulsory secondary education (ESO) in Spain.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• All 300 official citizenship test questions, explained in Spanish, Arabic, French, English, Romanian, Chinese and Ukrainian.
• Practice by topic: work on each of the 5 sections separately.
• Mock exam: 25 questions in 45 minutes with a score at the end, just like the real day.
• Spaced review of mistakes: the app brings back the questions you got wrong until you master them.
• Progress saved with your email account.
• Read-aloud: hear every question, handy if reading Spanish is still hard.
• Interface also available in Catalan.

FREE, NO SIGN-UP
Try 25 questions and 1 full mock exam without an account and without paying.

FULL ACCESS: €9.99 ONE-OFF PAYMENT
A single payment of €9.99 unlocks everything for 90 days: all questions, explanations and mock exams. It is not a subscription. Guarantee: if you fail after using the app, we refund your €9.99.

OFFICIAL SOURCE
Every question and answer comes from the CCSE 2026 preparation manual (Manual de preparación de la prueba CCSE 2026) published by the Instituto Cervantes at examenes.cervantes.es. The explanations and translations were generated with AI and reviewed before publication.

USEFUL EXAM FACTS
The exam fee is €85 and includes two attempts. Sittings are held on the last Thursday of every month except August and December. More than 98% of candidates pass the standard test, so with practice you can too. Always check dates and registration deadlines at examenes.cervantes.es.
```
