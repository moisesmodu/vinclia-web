## What is the CCSE?

The CCSE (*prueba de Conocimientos Constitucionales y Socioculturales de España*) is a multiple-choice test about the Spanish Constitution, institutions, geography, culture and everyday life. Foreigners applying for nationality by residence must pass it. The exceptions are people under 18, people whose legal capacity has been modified by a court, and those who completed compulsory secondary education (ESO) in Spain. Coming from a Spanish-speaking country does **not** exempt you from the CCSE; it only exempts you from the DELE A2 language exam.

Every exam question comes from a public bank of 300 questions published by the Instituto Cervantes in the [CCSE 2026 preparation manual](https://examenes.cervantes.es/sites/default/files/manual-ccse-2026-def.pdf). That is why practising with the real questions makes far more sense than using invented ones.

## 5 sample questions

**1. España es…** (Spain is…)
a) **una monarquía parlamentaria** (a parliamentary monarchy). b) una república federal (a federal republic). c) una monarquía federal (a federal monarchy).
The King is head of State, but political power lies with Parliament and the Government.

**2. ¿Quién es el jefe del Estado en España?** (Who is the head of State in Spain?)
a) El presidente del Gobierno (the Prime Minister). b) **El rey** (the King). c) El ministro de Economía (the Minister of Economy).

**3. En España hay una religión oficial.** (Spain has an official religion.)
Verdadero (true) / **Falso (false).**
Spain is a non-denominational State and guarantees freedom of religion.

**4. ¿Dónde están las islas Baleares?** (Where are the Balearic Islands?)
a) En el mar Cantábrico (in the Cantabrian Sea). b) **En el mar Mediterráneo** (in the Mediterranean Sea). c) En el océano Atlántico (in the Atlantic Ocean).

**5. ¿Cuál es el número de teléfono único para cualquier emergencia?** (What is the single phone number for any emergency?)
a) 060. b) **112.** c) 911.
911 is the emergency number in other countries; in Spain it is 112.

## The exam in brief

- **25 questions** drawn from the bank of 300, across 5 sections: government and civic participation (10), rights and duties (3, true or false), geography (2), culture and history (3) and Spanish society (7).
- **45 minutes** to answer.
- **Pass mark: 15 correct answers** (60%). Wrong answers do not subtract points.
- **Fee: 85 EUR**, which includes two attempts.
- Held on the last Thursday of every month except August and December. Always confirm dates at examenes.cervantes.es.

The exam guides are in Spanish, for example the [CCSE pass mark guide](/es/guia/nota-para-aprobar-ccse) and the [CCSE 2027 dates](/es/guia/examen-ccse-fechas-2027).

## FAQ

**Are these the same questions as in the real exam?**
Yes. We use the questions from the official 2026 manual of the Instituto Cervantes, which is where the 25 exam questions come from.

**Do I need to register to practise for free?**
No. The 25 free questions and the first mock exam work without an account. You only need an email address if you want to save your progress.

**Can I take the exam in English?**
No. The official exam is always in Spanish. What you can do in English is study the explanations. If you cannot read or write, there is an adapted version where the questions are read aloud; you must request it when you register.

**Is it hard to pass?**
More than 98% of candidates pass the standard test. You only need 15 out of 25, so preparing with the official bank gives you an excellent chance.
